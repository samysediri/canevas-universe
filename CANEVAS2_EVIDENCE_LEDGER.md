# CANEVAS 2.0 — EVIDENCE LEDGER

Status: working scientific register. This file separates results that strengthen a quantitative claim from mere compatibility, underdetermination, and failed/corrected ideas. It is intended to become the structural backbone of a later academic rewrite of the 2024 book.

## Status scale

- **A — Quantitative support for a narrow claim:** a preregistered or otherwise controlled calculation established the stated narrow result. This is not automatically evidence for Canevas as a whole.
- **B — Physical/mathematical compatibility:** the book's intuition has a legitimate analogue in known mathematics/physics, but no Canevas-specific prediction was established.
- **C — Underdetermined / open:** the question is meaningful, but the answer depends on a measure, prior, unknown physical rate, or missing model component.
- **D — Corrected / rejected / closed:** the original strong formulation failed, was shown non-robust, or a candidate positive result was traced to methodology/computation.

## 1. Infinity and eventual realization

**Original intuition:** infinite opportunity implies eventual realization of physically possible configurations.

**Finding:** infinity alone is insufficient. Eventual occurrence depends on the probability sequence and dependence structure; for independent trials, divergence/convergence of sums such as `sum p_n` separates important regimes.

**Status:** **A for the mathematical correction; D for the original unconditional claim.**

**Keep in Canevas 2.0:** an infinite or unbounded opportunity structure does not by itself imply that every possibility occurs. A physical measure and recurrence/opportunity law are required.

## 2. Observer typicality / human birth rank

**Original intuition:** an ordinary observer should be born near the middle of the finite human-birth distribution, potentially constraining humanity's future size.

**Finding:** broad centrality is generic under many rank measures, but exact median proximity is not privileged under a uniform-rank model. Posterior future-human totals change strongly under SSA/SIA and prior choice. Calendar extinction dates are not obtained without an independent demographic future model.

**Status:** **C.**

**Rejected strong claim:** the user's birth date/rank does not robustly predict an end-of-humanity date.

**Keep:** observer typicality exposes a genuine measure problem that any observer-containing cosmology must define explicitly.

## 3. Temporal intuition / future-conditioned present

**Original intuition:** future and present may be interdependent rather than only past -> future.

**D1.1 control:** standard Aharonov-Bergmann-Lebowitz style conditioning can make an intermediate conditional distribution depend on a future postselection while ordinary Born statistics are recovered when the future result is ignored.

**D2:** the 2024 text supplies none of the quantitative ingredients needed for a distinct physical model: no explicit state space, dynamics, probability law, observable map, or numerical departure from standard QM.

**Status:** **B.**

**Keep:** conceptual compatibility with time-symmetric quantum conditioning.

**Do not claim:** retrocausal signalling, new quantum mechanics, or empirical evidence for Canevas.

---

## 4. Finite local information capacity / global structure

**E1:** conditional finite entropy gives a finite exponential state-count scale.

**E2:** a published cosmic-horizon entropy scale of order `10^122 k_B` provides a concrete physical analogue of enormous but finite local information capacity.

**Status:** **B, with A-level support for the narrow conditional mathematics.**

**Keep:** local finiteness can coexist conceptually with a globally larger or potentially unbounded architecture.

**Not derived:** global infinity, exact finite Hilbert-space dimension, literal duplicate universes, or recurrence time.

## 5. Boltzmann-brain / coherent-history branch

**Finding:** persistent dynamics can mathematically favor coherent histories over fluctuation histories in toy models. Whether ordinary observers dominate Boltzmann-brain-like fluctuations depends on cosmological rates, lifetimes, and framework assumptions.

**Status:** **B/C.**

**Keep:** history/coherence can matter; observer comparison must use physically motivated production rates and lifetimes.

**Do not claim:** Canevas has solved the Boltzmann-brain problem.

---

## 6. Multiple domains / bubble-universe analogues

**Finding:** external-inflation/bubble-collision style models provide physically testable analogues of multiple-domain ideas. Some parameter regions are observationally constrained; absence of collisions does not by itself exclude all such domains.

**Status:** **B/C.**

**Keep:** multiple-domain intuitions can be instantiated in falsifiable physics.

**Do not identify:** Canevas with eternal inflation unless an independent mapping is derived.

## 7. Fermi / civilization finitude branch

**B1:** radio silence alone constrains combinations such as rarity x active lifetime, not lifetime uniquely.

**B2:** in the tested settlement model family, long-lived expansion can cross the Galaxy on timescales much shorter than Galactic age.

**B3:** older-world age leads can leave substantial time for settlement even after Gyr-scale extra biological/technological delays in many sensitivity cases.

**B4:** if the effective number of independent opportunities is large, a null observation forces the combined success probability per opportunity to be very small.

**B5:** a very large astrophysical pool of potentially habitable rocky worlds exists, but converting that pool into an effective civilization opportunity count remains dominated by the unknown conversion factor.

**Status:** **A for several narrow conditional calculations; C for the physical interpretation.**

**Keep:** there must be at least one strong bottleneck in the chain if the effective opportunity count is large.

**Do not claim:** the bottleneck is necessarily civilization mortality or that Fermi proves the book's finitude claim.

## 8. Dark-matter/baryon ratio zeta branch

### Historical signal
Earlier CLASS + halo/cooling proxies sometimes produced finite interior optima in the same order of magnitude as the observed dark-matter/baryon ratio. Historical robustness runs showed the preferred peak moving substantially with cosmic epoch.

### C2-M1
Retrospective audit found strong epoch-measure dependence. This was explicitly post-hoc and cannot count as new evidence.

### C2-M2
A proper-time weighted integration recipe failed its preregistered robustness threshold because late-time weight dominated; removing z=2 shifted the median strongly.

### C2-M3
A blind cumulative baryon-opportunity proxy produced its maximum at the first numerically valid zeta, not a validated interior optimum.

### C2-M4
All 33 low-zeta failures occurred at the same CLASS matter-power stage and all 33 were rescued by a high-baryon diagnostic cap. Therefore the M3 lower boundary cannot be treated as an anthropic/physical selection boundary.

**Status:** **D for the claim that this current proxy robustly selects a finite observed-like zeta; C for the broader physical question.**

**Keep:** the dark-matter/baryon tradeoff remains a scientifically meaningful astrophysical question.

**Close:** no further tuning of this proxy family to recover an observed-like optimum. A future revisit requires a separately motivated galaxy-formation/observer model and a new preregistration.

## 9. Central measure problem

Across independent branches, the same obstacle repeatedly appears:

`P(theta | O) proportional to P(O | theta) * mu(theta)`

The result depends crucially on what `mu` counts and how observer opportunity `P(O | theta)` is defined. Epoch weighting, SSA/SIA, cosmological priors, observer lifetime, and event-counting conventions can materially alter conclusions.

**Status:** **C, but this is currently the central scientific problem of Canevas 2.0.**

**Research target:** derive a measure from independently motivated physics rather than selecting it because it makes our observed universe look typical.

# Current Canevas 2.0 core

The scientifically defensible core is now narrower than the 2024 book:

1. A space of possible physical domains/configurations may be considered, but its measure must be explicitly defined.
2. Observer-conditioned predictions require both a physical prior/measure and a physically justified observer/opportunity model.
3. Infinite opportunity does not imply universal realization without a probability/dependence law.
4. Local finite information capacity is compatible with, but does not establish, a globally unbounded architecture.
5. Observer typicality is a derived statistical question, not an axiom.
6. Consciousness is not presently derived as a fundamental physical variable and should remain outside the quantitative axioms until a measurable bridge exists.

# Claims currently excluded from the scientific core

- The user's birth rank predicts a calendar end of humanity.
- Infinite existence alone makes consciousness inevitable.
- Fermi silence proves civilizations are short-lived.
- Delayed-choice/postselection proves future-to-past signalling.
- Current zeta proxy calculations predict the observed dark-matter/baryon ratio.
- Finite local entropy proves an infinite Canevas or literal duplicate universes.

# Criterion for promotion to stronger evidence

A future Canevas result may be promoted only if:

1. the mathematical model and parameter choices are independently motivated;
2. target observations are withheld from model selection where feasible;
3. success/failure criteria are frozen before output;
4. reasonable controls and alternative parameterizations are tested;
5. numerical/computational boundaries are separated from physical exclusions;
6. a successful result predicts something quantitatively more specific than standard physics or an unconstrained anthropic explanation.
