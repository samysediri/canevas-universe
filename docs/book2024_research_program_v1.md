# CANEVAS — Book 2024 Anchored Research Program v1

Purpose: keep future work anchored to *Un Regard Probabiliste sur L'origine de la Conscience* (2024) rather than drifting toward attractive but weakly connected side hypotheses.

## Source-derived core propositions

### B24-P1 — Existence necessity
Book claim: non-existence is impossible; existence must exist.
Status: metaphysical axiom. Not directly empirical unless paired with a physical model producing differential predictions.

### B24-P2 — Infinite generative field
Book claim: existence is an infinite field of energy/random phenomena in which every possibility eventually occurs.
Status: mathematically formalizable. Key question: under what stochastic assumptions does infinite opportunity imply eventual occurrence with probability 1?

### B24-P3 — Consciousness/existence linkage
Book claim: consciousness and existence are inseparable; consciousness persists as a fundamental aspect without requiring autobiographical memory continuity.
Status: requires an operational definition of observer-moments before empirical testing. Must not use quantum 'observation' as evidence of human consciousness without an independent mechanism.

### B24-P4 — Inevitability of intelligent civilizations
Book claim: in an infinite generative field, intelligent civilizations eventually arise.
Status: conditional consequence of B24-P2 if the probability measure for civilization-producing histories is nonzero and the recurrence assumptions hold.

### B24-P5 — Finite technological civilizations
Book claim: intelligent civilizations undergo rapid growth, peak, then decline/end; finite civilization windows may help explain Fermi silence.
Status: empirical/modeling target. Candidate data: exoplanet occurrence, SETI/technosignature null results, Galactic colonization timescales, civilization lifetime priors.

### B24-P6 — Infinite succession of finite conscious episodes/civilizations
Book claim: existence may consist of infinitely many finite cycles, avoiding a globally terminal conscious state.
Status: formalizable once recurrence/generative dynamics and observer identity are defined.

### B24-P7 — Future/present linkage
Book claim: future conditions may influence or constrain the present; time may be globally interconnected rather than purely one-way.
Status: needs a mathematical time-symmetric or two-boundary model and an observable differing from ordinary forward-causal predictions.

### B24-P8 — Self-location datum
Book claim: being human, conscious, born in 1992 near a technologically dense population era is potentially informative.
Status: hold out as a downstream test. Do not use it to select observer measure, priors, reference class, or model parameters.

## Research order

1. B24-A1: formalize the 'everything possible eventually happens' claim.
2. B24-A2: adversarial recurrence / Boltzmann-observer problem: determine whether generic fluctuations swamp structured evolutionary observers.
3. B24-B1: finite-civilization/Fermi model using external astronomical data and preregistered priors.
4. B24-C1: derive a concrete cosmological process from the infinite generative field and identify an observable signature.
5. B24-D1: construct a time-symmetric/two-boundary model and identify a discriminating experiment.
6. B24-E1: only after an independent observer ontology exists, reopen self-location and the 1992 datum.

## Methodological locks

- Negative results are retained.
- No model is retuned under the same label after seeing its output.
- Observed target values are hidden whenever possible until a prediction is frozen.
- A philosophical axiom is not called empirically validated merely because a downstream toy model passes.
- Every inference chain must distinguish: BOOK AXIOM -> MATHEMATICAL BRIDGE -> PHYSICAL MODEL -> OBSERVABLE -> DATA.
- If a bridge is not derived, mark it explicitly as an extra premise.
